Project Update —

Shoshin: The Path of Ascension

Our team at Pelican House Games  remains busy behind-the-scenes working hard to get Shoshin: The Path of Ascension onto everyone’s tabletop!


We wanted to share some of our milestones and momemtum with our burdgeoning community of gamers.

Mark your calendars as we’ve set our official Kickstarter launch for October 23rd!


We’ve also planned a three-year roadmap featuring two game expansions to deepen the Shoshin ecosystem.

 

1. Community Growth

Your support continues to amaze us! Our community is growing every day, and we’ve now reached:

  • 300+ Kickstarter Followers

  • 500+ Facebook Followers

  • 400+ Instagram Followers


Every new supporter brings us closer to launch—thank you!

2. Meet our Lead Illustrator!

We’re excited to welcome Samantha Burk as our Lead Illustrator!


Samantha witnessed our demos at GenCon and immediately fell in love. After several conversations she decided to join our team to help us bring our ultimate vision to life.


Graphic Designer, Illustrator, Character Artist, and Avid Game Enthusiast. With a passion for blending narrative and design, she will add a bold and enduring style to the game’s visual identity. Her talent and vision are shaping Shoshin’s ukiyo-e-inspired direction.
