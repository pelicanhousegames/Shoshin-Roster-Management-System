Asset Rules & Mechanics

Game Mechanic Description
Detachment & Operation At the start of the round, one or more engaged friendly units may form an Ozutsu detachment. Only units in this detachment may move, reload, or fire the cannon.
Collective Activation When the Ozutsu activates, the detachment moves and acts as a single unit, but each model in the detachment remains an individual target for attacks and effects.
Firing Arc & Derived Stats The Ozutsu may only fire in the direction its muzzle faces at the end of the detachment’s movement. Attack and Movement are derived from the detachment’s engaged units, and Initiative checks use the highest Initiative among them.
Captain & Crew A vessel may activate only if a Suieijutsu-trained Captain and the required Crew were aboard at the start of the round. Only Captain & Crew may operate the vessel during activation. Passengers do not require training and may act normally unless restricted by movement or Sprinting.
Collective Activation & Sprinting Whenever the vessel Moves, Captain & Crew cannot move or act independently. Passengers may act normally unless the vessel Sprints. If the vessel does not move, all units aboard may act freely. If the vessel Sprints, its Movement is doubled and all passengers forfeit their actions for the round.
Contested & Initiative If the Captain or any required Crew are engaged by an enemy unit before the vessel has moved, the vessel is contested and cannot activate. When Initiative is required, use the Captain’s Initiative; this does not affect (duplicate) Initiative Phase turn order.
