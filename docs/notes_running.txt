Atakebune (Large): The biggest, heavily armed, and often armored warships used for transporting samurai, featuring large, castle-like box structures. Oda Nobunaga famously used iron-covered versions called tekkōsen.
Sekibune (Medium): The most common, standard naval warship in the middle of the Sengoku period, which was faster than the Atakebune.
Kobaya (Small): Small, fast, oar-powered boats used for scouting, signaling, and rapid attacks, often called "small boats". 




The Three-Tier Hierarchy
The Japanese naval system categorized its fleet based on size, function, and speed. You can think of it as a pyramid structure:
Class 	Type	Primary Role	Key Feature
O-fune (Large)	Atakebune	Floating Fortresses	Defensive power and psychological warfare; often had iron plating.
Chu-fune (Medium)	Sekibune	Main Line Warship	Balanced speed and firepower; the "destroyer" of the fleet.
Ko-fune (Small)	Kobaya	Utility & Skirmish	High maneuverability; used for scouting and messenger duties.
